## Welcome

Welcome to Acme Corp. This handbook outlines our values, workplace expectations, and how policies connect across the organization.

## Our Values

- **Integrity** — Act ethically in all dealings.
- **Collaboration** — Cross-functional teamwork is expected.
- **Customer focus** — Decisions should consider end-user impact.

## Working Hours

Standard full-time employees work 40 hours per week, Monday through Friday. Flexible hours may be approved by your manager. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM – 3:00 PM local time.

## Code of Conduct

Harassment, discrimination, and retaliation are prohibited. Report concerns to HR or via the anonymous ethics hotline.
