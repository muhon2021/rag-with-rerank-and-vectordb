# Leave Policy

**Document ID:** LEAVE-POL-2025  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Owner:** People Operations

## Overview

This policy defines paid time off for full-time employees at Acme Corp.

## Annual Leave (Vacation)

Full-time employees receive **20 days of paid annual leave** per calendar year, accrued monthly (1.67 days per month).

- Leave must be requested at least **5 business days** in advance for periods of 3+ consecutive days.
- Unused annual leave may **carry over up to 5 days** into the next calendar year; excess is forfeited unless local law requires otherwise.
- Part-time employees receive pro-rated annual leave based on FTE percentage.

## Sick Leave

Employees receive **10 days of paid sick leave** per year. Sick leave does not carry over.

## Public Holidays

Acme Corp observes 10 company-wide public holidays per year (list published each December).

## Parental Leave

Eligible employees receive up to **12 weeks paid parental leave** following birth or adoption. Contact HR to initiate.

## Request Process

Submit leave requests via the HR portal (Workday). Managers must approve within 3 business days.

## Contractors

Contractors are **not eligible** for Acme Corp paid annual leave. Refer to staffing agency agreements.
